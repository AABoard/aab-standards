# Contributing to AAB Standards

This repository supports public, source-traced development of AAB standards. Contributions should improve clarity, developmental appropriateness, evidence traceability, or review process quality.

## Contribution Types

- Standards comments on draft clauses.
- Proposed clause language.
- Grade-band progression suggestions.
- Evidence trace additions or corrections.
- Terminology and taxonomy proposals.
- Response-to-comments improvements.
- Draft templates and review process improvements.

## Submission Expectations

For standards comments, include:

- The affected draft, clause, or topic.
- The age band or education context affected.
- The problem with the current language.
- Proposed revision, if available.
- Evidence source, registry record, or rationale.
- Whether the issue is editorial, technical, developmental, ethical, or governance-related.

## Review Principles

- Keep K-12 developmental progression explicit.
- Separate descriptive evidence from normative requirements.
- Avoid product, provider, or platform endorsement.
- Use source-traced claims.
- Preserve limitations and uncertainty.
- Do not treat a public registry record as a final AAB position without review.

## Pull Request Checklist

- [ ] Clause or topic is identified.
- [ ] Rationale is included.
- [ ] Evidence trace is included when a claim is changed.
- [ ] Public-facing language is neutral and non-endorsing.
- [ ] Response-to-comments or change notes are updated for major revisions.
