## Decision Categories

- `accepted`: change adopted substantially as proposed.
- `revised`: concern accepted but wording or implementation differs.
- `rejected`: not adopted, with rationale.
- `deferred`: valid issue but requires later evidence, scope, or governance decision.

## Review Notes

Record why each decision was made, especially when comments involve developmental appropriateness, evidence limits, public-risk issues, or possible endorsement language.
